# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, sexualized language, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable, in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that don't follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge
inappropriate.

Report problems to the maintainer at navneetforus@gmail.com. Reports are
handled confidentially.
